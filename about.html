Who we are

Built by engineers,
for engineering teams.

QAI Corp. is a boutique quality engineering agency with a single obsession: helping SaaS, e-commerce, and fintech teams ship reliable software faster through expert QA and the intelligent application of automation.

Our story

Why QAI Corp. exists

We've seen firsthand what happens when quality is treated as an afterthought: releases delayed, production incidents at 2 AM, enterprise clients churning because of preventable bugs, and engineering teams demoralized by endless firefighting.

We started QAI Corp. because we believed there was a better way — a systematic, senior-led approach to QA that could give engineering teams back their confidence and velocity, without the overhead of hiring a full-time team.

Our mission is to apply that same spirit of rigorous, evidence-based thinking to the problem of software quality — for teams that can't afford to slow down.

🎯

Our Mission

To make reliable software delivery the default state for every engineering team we work with — regardless of their size, stack, or starting point.

🔭

Our Vision

A world where "it works in production" is not a milestone — it's a baseline. Where quality is engineered in, not bolted on as an afterthought.

Our Approach

Senior-led quality engineering, delivered from the same time zone as your team — no overnight handoffs, no waiting a day for an answer. We combine deep QA expertise with modern automation tooling to deliver outcomes a solo freelancer or an unmanaged offshore team can't match.
